How these estimates are made

Names and locations come from the team workbook. Walking estimates use 2 minutes within the same building, 6 minutes within the same campus area, or 12 minutes between areas, plus 0.5 minutes per floor difference rounded up. Multi-floor locations use ground level as a placeholder. These are coarse synthetic assumptions, not measured routes. The workbook’s area labels are used as supplied; entrances, hills, stairs and lifts are not modelled.

Restaurant waits are manually assigned scenarios: longer during lunch, shorter during quieter periods. They include queueing, ordering and food preparation. Opening hours and venue-specific accuracy have not been checked.

Approximate timings are fine. Do not include eating time. No name or student ID is requested.

Times include walking to the restaurant, waiting until food arrives, and walking to your destination. Eating time is excluded. Walking times are assumed equal in both directions. Waiting estimates use your arrival time at each restaurant. Estimates cover 09:00–19:00 and do not verify opening hours or live queues. Vending machines assume zero waiting time; they may offer snacks rather than a full meal.
